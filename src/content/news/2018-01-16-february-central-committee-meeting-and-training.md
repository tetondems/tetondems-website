All registered Democrats are welcome to attend the Central Committee Meeting in Laramie from February 23 to 25. See below for an outline of the schedule. For more info go to [http://www.wyodems.org](http://www.wyodems.org/)

**Friday, February 23**, Welcome Reception at the Cheyenne Botanic Gardens. 6 PM. Purchase tickets [HERE](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/laramie-county-democratic-legislative-reception-tickets-41886062340?aff=efbeventtix).

**Saturday, February 24** – all-day - Democratic National Training Committee. [DNTC trainers](https://www.traindemocrats.org/meet-the-team) will cover organizing, online communication, messaging, and fundraising for county party officers, activists, candidates and anyone else who wants to help elect Democrats in 2018. - $25 suggested donation.

The annual Nellie Tayloe Ross Dinner, 6 PM, in the Ballroom of the Plains Hotel. Purchase tickets [HERE](https://act.myngp.com/Forms/115816558181615616).

**Sunday, February 25 -** 9 a.m. - Central Committee Meeting - FREE

If you are interested in carpooling please e-mail info@tetondems.org
