Right before starting our May monthly meeting precinct officers elected a new State Committeeman. We thank Mike Welch for all his year of service in various Teton Dem Leadership positions and we look forward to working with his as a volunteer.

"My name is John Goodwin and I live in Wilson, WY and work at Jackson Hole Mountain Resort. I am an avid backcountry skier, golfer, and fly fisherman. Prior to moving to Teton County I lived in Washington, DC and worked on Capitol Hill for 4 years for Peter Welch (D-VT). I look forward to working with my fellow Democrats here in Teton County and the rest of Wyoming to strengthen the party and help Democrats win local and statewide elections in the years to come. "

The State Committeeman is an elected position to serve as the Teton Democrats State Party Liaison. The State Committeeman and State Committeewoman work across the state with the other county Democratic Parties.
