The April 13 County Caucus + Delegate Selection will be held at the Board of County Commissioners' Chambers (200 S. Willow Street) at 10 am.

Run for or elect delegates to the June State Convention in Casper, WY. Vote on platform issues.

Doors open at 9:15a and close. at 9:45a. There are 25 Teton County delegate positions to attend the state convention. We will also caucus for Presidential selection.

Participants need to be registered Dems by the end of the County Clerk business day on April 9, 2024. Register at the County Admin. building at Willow and Simpson, 9 a.m.- 5 p.m., Monday - Friday. You can verify your registration by phone 733-7733.

NOTE: If you are 18 years old on or by election day, November 5th, you can attend and vote at the Convention. You must pre-register as a Democrat at the County Clerk’s office by April 9, 2024.
