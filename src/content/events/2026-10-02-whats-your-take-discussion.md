The Teton County Democratic Party hosts its first "What's your take" discussion of local and some state issues. Topics range from housing to public lands, the impact of property taxes, and the expected role of local government, plus anything else you would like to discuss.

This is an informal, non-partisan discussion, open to everyone, and all candidates are invited.

The goal is an informal setting to discuss local issues face to face with candidates and voters, away from the digital world and council and board rooms. Julien Hass, Teton County Dems Secretary, is personally inviting all candidates and anyone else who would like to join.
