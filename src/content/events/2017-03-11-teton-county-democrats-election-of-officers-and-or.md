State by-laws require the election of new officers in March of odd years. We have scheduled this year’s meeting on Saturday, March 11th at 4:00 PM at the County Commissioner chambers at 200 S. Willow. All Democrats are welcome, but only the precinct committee people have the power to vote on officers.

Want to provide input and learn what the Teton County Democrats goals are for the future? Join us!

Agenda:
\*Legislative Reports by Wyoming Reps. Schwartz & Gierau
\*Opportunities for Action
\*Election of 2017 Officers by Precinct Captains
