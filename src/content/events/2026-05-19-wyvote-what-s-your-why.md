**Teton County Library Ordway Auditorium**

**Presented by Gail Symons of** [**WYVOTE.VOTE**](http://wyvote.vote/) and ***The League of Women Voters of Wyoming***

**In Wyoming’s 2024 Primary Election, just 27% of eligible voters cast a ballot. That means nearly 3 out of 4 Wyoming voices went unheard at a critical moment that helped shape our state’s future. We can do better!**

**Everyone’s voice should be heard, and in Wyoming, each vote matters more than you think. WY VOTE ’26 is a non-partisan grassroots campaign focused on raising awareness about the importance of Wyoming’s elections. Come to this free, non-partisan presentation to learn more about Wyoming's 2026 elections. Gail Symons will provide insights, perspectives, and ideas. She is a columnist for the Cowboy State Daily, a League of Women Voters of WY Board Member, and developer of the** [**WYVOTE.VOTE**](http://wyvote.vote/) **website.**

**Join us for this informative WY VOTE presentation on Tuesday, 5/19 @ 5:00 pm in the Teton County Library. Hope to see you there!**
