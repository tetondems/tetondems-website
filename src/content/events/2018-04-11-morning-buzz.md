Please join us at 8 a.m. on April 11th at Pizzeria Caldera to talk about grassroots organizing for the 2018 elections. Candidates, special interest groups, activists and lovers of Democracy, please join us to discuss how well-organized canvassing and data collection will determine who wins in 2018 and beyond. In Wyoming, forty percent of eligible voters don’t go to the polls on election day. It is critical that we find out why. If you want to join the conversation well-prepared please read this NYT article, bring your coffee and let’s talk.
