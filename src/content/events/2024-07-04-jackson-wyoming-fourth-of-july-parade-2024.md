Join us in Jackson, Wyoming's Fourth of July Parade to make a strong statement that democracy and freedom of choice are woven into the fabric of our country. We will be marching forward – not backward.

Meet beginning at 9 a.m. at the Teton County Fairgrounds to socialize and design posters highlighting critical issues for our country and commmunity, from public lands and affordable housing, to fair wages and of course, reproductive freedom. The parade starts at 10:30 a.m.

The route travels down Snow King Avenue to Glenwood, turning on Broadway, and ending on Willow Street.

Your own family friendly signs are encouraged. Please walk with our local candidates to send an undeniable message that we will not lay down as the freedoms we've gained over 248 years are rolled back.

If you'd like to help us promote and decorate in advance of the parade, please email us at [info@tetondems.org](mailto:info@tetondems.org).
