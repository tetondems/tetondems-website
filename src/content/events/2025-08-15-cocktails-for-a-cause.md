**Join Planned Parenthood for Cocktails for a Cause — an event to celebrate the work of Planned Parenthood of the Rocky Mountains (PPRM) in the region. These are challenging times for reproductive health care and bodily autonomy. Listen as CEO, Adrienne Mansanares, discusses the current landscape of reproductive health and abortion care in Wyoming and the broader region. Come out to connect with like-minded individuals and be in solidarity with our mission to care, no matter what.**
