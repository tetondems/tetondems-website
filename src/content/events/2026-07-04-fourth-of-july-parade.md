**Come join us marching in the 4th of July Parade!**

This July 4th is a special one-not only is it the **250th anniversary of the birth of our Nation,** the United States of America, it is also an election year. It is a **DRAMATICALLY** important election year. This is our opportunity to show our support for our Democracy, this is how we show our strength. This is how we show we are still here, for GOOD, and not going anywhere.

Help us swell our numbers by walking with us-a few minutes, or the whole parade! Invite your friends, bring your favorite pet(s)! We will also have local and state politicians walking with us, and the rumor has it that we will have some **candidates for national office** marching with us!
