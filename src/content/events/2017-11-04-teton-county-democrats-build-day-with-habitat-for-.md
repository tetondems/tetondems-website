On November 4th the Teton County Democrats will be partnering with Teton Habitat and the Rotary Club of Jackson Hole for a build day. Spend a day giving back, and come anytime between 10 AM and 4 PM.

We also have set a fundraising goal of $1,500. All funds go directly to the Habitat for Humanity Branch in Teton County. Donations from $5 to $100 or more, will help us reach out fundraising goal and support a great non-profit in our county.

Light refreshments will be provided. Please dress warmly.

To help us get a sense of how many people are coming please sign up here or send us an e-mail at info@tetondems.org or online at [https://goo.gl/QuUN5s](https://goo.gl/QuUN5s).
