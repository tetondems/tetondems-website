The Sublette County Democratic Party hosts "Meet, Greet, Eat with Wyoming Democratic Candidates," a fundraising event open to the public, at the Sublette County Library, Pinedale branch.

- **Candidates:** James Byrd (U.S. Senate), Gavin Lobmeyer (House District 7), and Ana Cordova (State Superintendent).
- **Also attending:** guest speaker Rah Reinholz and Wyoming Democratic Party Chair Lucas Fralick.
- **Free meal**, plus a raffle fundraiser with multiple prizes, including baskets of local-made goods, a guided river tour, and a complimentary stay at a historic location. Ticket prices and prizes will be announced by the Sublette County Democratic Party.

Learn more on the [Sublette County Democrats site](https://sublette.wyodems.net/) or [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/SubletteWyoDemParty).
