Off Square Theatre Company and the Teton County Library Foundation & Friends, in collaboration with the Center for the Arts, present a free staged reading of Ray Bradbury's *Fahrenheit 451* on the closing day of Banned Books Week.

The reading runs about 100 minutes and is followed by an audience discussion with panelists including director Anne Mason, HD-16 State Representative Mike Yin, and librarians Melissa Snider and Terri Lesley.

- **Free.** Tickets are available through the [Center's website](https://www.jhcenterforthearts.org/event/fahrenheit-451/).
- ASL interpretation will be provided.
- Questions: Center for the Arts box office, 307-733-4900.
