The Center for the Arts, in collaboration with the Teton County Library and Off Square Theatre Company, presents a free staged reading of Ray Bradbury's *Fahrenheit 451*. A discussion follows the reading, with several panelists including HD-16 State Representative Mike Yin.

Free.
