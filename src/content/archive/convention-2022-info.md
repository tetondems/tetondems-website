## 2022 Teton County Democratic Convention

To elect delegates to the state convention, and adopt a county platform and resolutions.

#### Saturday, April 16, 2022, 4 p.m.

#### County Commissioners Chambers
Teton County Administration Building, 2nd Floor
200 S Willow Street
Jackson, WY 83001
[https://bit.ly/tetondems2020 ](https://bit.ly/tetondems2020 )

You must be registered as a Democrat in Teton County **4 days prior to the convention** to attend.

If you are a Teton County Democrat interested in serving as a delegate at the Wyoming State Democratic Convention in Rock Springs on June 11 and 12, 2022, please [apply here](/delegate-candidacy-form) OR nominations can be made on the day of the convention. You must have been registered as a Democrat **15 days prior to the convention** to be a delegate.

Teton County will send up to 24 delegates to the state convention.

Please [review the proposed 2022 platform](/2022-platform) prior to the April 16 convention. Email your suggested amendments and revisions to [officers@tetondems.org](mailto:officers@tetondems.org).

Emailed comments will be the most prompt and effective way to hear your voice.

#### Convention Agenda:

1\. Call to order by the County/Convention Chair.

2\. Report of the Credentials and Ballots Committee regarding credentials and qualifications of the delegates.

3\. Election of delegates to the State Convention.

4\. Report of the Resolutions and Platform Committee and adoption of a County Platform.

5\. Other Business.

6\. Adjournment.
