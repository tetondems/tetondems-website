## 2020 Virtual Teton County Democratic Convention

To elect delegates to the state convention, and adopt a county platform and resolutions.

#### Saturday, May 23, 3 p.m.

#### Register for the Convention (on Zoom) at [https://bit.ly/tetondems2020](https://bit.ly/tetondems2020 )

Please register individually even if multiple voters are in the same household. We will use the polling feature of Zoom. You must be registered as a Democrat 15 days prior to the convention to attend.

If you are a Teton County Democrat interested in serving as a delegate to the Wyoming State Democratic Convention, please [apply here](/delegate-candidacy-form) OR nominations can be made on the day of the convention.

Teton County will send 21 delegates to the state convention.

Presidential Nominee Joe Biden – 14 delegates

Presidential Nominee Bernie Sanders – 7 delegates

Please review the [Proposed 2020 Platform](/2020-platform) prior to the May 23rd convention. Email your suggested amendments and revisions to [officers@tetondems.org](mailto:officers@tetondems.org).

Emailed comments will be the most prompt and effective way to hear your voice in this year’s unconventional convention. Day-of debate will be limited.

Convention Agenda:

-   Call to Order

-   Pledge of Allegiance, Opening Remarks & Recognitions

-   Report of the Credentials & Ballots Committee

-   Results of the Caucus and Allocation of Delegates Are Announced

-   Election of Delegates to the State Convention

-   Consideration of the Platform & Resolutions

-   Other Business & Adjournment
