## 2024 Wyoming State Democratic Convention Delegate Nomination Form

We are accepting candidates for Teton County’s 25 delegates to the 2022 Wyoming State Democratic Convention in Casper on June 1. Fill out the form to below to submit your nomination. You must have been registered as Democrat in Teton County **15 days prior to the April 13 county convention** to qualify.

Assistance is available to help with travel, lodging and meal costs
