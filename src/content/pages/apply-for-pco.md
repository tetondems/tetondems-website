## Precinct Leaders

Want to get more involved with the Party? Run to become a Precinct Committee Officer (PCO), the Democratic representative for your precinct. PCOs are the grassroots organizing base for everything we do.

- [Find your precinct](https://gis.tetoncountywy.gov/portal/apps/instant/sidebar/index.html?appid=17efecf67dfa4df2990aef473784a0df) on the county map.
- [Download the application](/files/Precinct-Person-Application-by-Party-Primary.pdf) (PDF).
- See who holds each seat now, and which are open, on [Who We Are](/who-we-are).

### What PCOs do

PCOs play an important role in elections. It's their job to get to know their neighbors, educate undecided or swing voters, and make sure Democrats are registered to vote. Before Election Day, they work to **turn out voters**.

PCOs are also a central part of the local Democratic organization. They elect Party leadership, help fill vacancies in elected office, and lead their precinct caucus every two years. **Being active with the Teton Dems will make you a more effective PCO.**

### How PCOs are chosen

PCOs are elected at the **primary election in August of even-numbered years**. Each precinct elects one committeeman and one committeewoman. Candidates file with the Teton County Clerk during the candidate filing period in May of that year, using the application above, and must live in the precinct.

The 2026 primary has already been held; the next PCO election is **August 2028**. Seats that are vacant between elections are filled by appointment. If you're interested in an open seat now, email [info@tetondems.org](mailto:info@tetondems.org).
