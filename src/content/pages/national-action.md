## Easy Steps Everyone can take

-   Vote in Federal Elections

-   Stay informed on federal bills

-   Contact members of Congress (5 calls)

-   Participate in Survey/polls (issue based national advocacy groups)


* * *

## Moderate steps for small change

-   Donate to Campaigns (Federal candidates or PACs)

-   Join National Organizations (Issue-based National Movements)

-   Organize advocacy efforts (coordinate letter or call campaigns)

-   Attend rallies or marches (Regional or National Events)


* * *

## Hard tasks that make greatest change

-   Work for a national campaign (paid or volunteer roles)

-   Run for Federal office (US House/Senate)
