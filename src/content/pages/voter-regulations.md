## Voter Regulations & Updates
**Same-day registration allowed**:

-   Voters can register and vote at the same time (either at polls or absentee site)


-   **Absentee voting**:


-   Requests open **January 1, 2026**

-   Available in-person or by request before each election


-   **Late registration rule**:


-   During the ~2 weeks before each election, registration requires **immediate voting**


-   **Updates to voter info**:


-   Address must be handled **in person** after deadlines

-   Party change deadline is **May 13th, 2026**


-   **General guidance**:


-   Registering early is recommended so your name appears on poll lists
