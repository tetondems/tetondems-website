## General election: Tuesday, November 3, 2026

| Date | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Tuesday, October 6** | Absentee voting begins. Vote early in person at the Election Center, County Administration Building, 200 S. Willow St., Jackson, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. weekdays, or request a mail ballot. |
| **Tuesday, October 20** | Last day to register in advance. |
| **October 20 to November 2** | You can still register, but you must vote at the same time, at the Election Center. |
| **Monday, November 2** | Last day of in-person absentee voting and last day to request a mail ballot. |
| **Tuesday, November 3** | Election Day. Vote centers open 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. Same-day registration is allowed. Mail ballots must **reach** the Clerk by 7 p.m. |

The Clerk's [absentee ballot request form](http://www.tetoncountywy.gov/DocumentCenter/View/5506/Request-for-Wyoming-Absentee-Ballot) takes a minute. Mail within the county can take a week, so send ballots back early or use the drop box outside the County Administration Building.

### Where to vote on Election Day

Any of the five vote centers, regardless of where you live in the county:

- [Teton County Library](https://goo.gl/maps/NPNE7BNAE11vR9MT8), 125 Virginian Ln, Jackson
- Teton County / Town of Jackson Recreation Center, 220 N. King St, Jackson
- [Old Wilson Schoolhouse Community Center](https://goo.gl/maps/jtzV4HZTXE2NH9mP9), 5655 Main St, Wilson
- [Teton County Weed & Pest Building](https://goo.gl/maps/eLL4iN9Eek1XXmzb8), 7575 US-89, Jackson
- [Alta Branch Library](https://goo.gl/maps/xceeRDmoEHRGkq3g7), 50 Alta School Rd, Alta

Confirm locations before you go on the Clerk's [Where to Vote on Election Day](https://www.tetoncountywy.gov/1927/Where-to-Vote-on-Election-Day) page.

## Rules that apply to every election

- **Registration closes 14 days before** each election. After that, you can still register but must vote at the same time.
- **Absentee voting opens 28 days before** each election, in person at the Election Center or by mail.
- **Address or party changes** after registration closes must be done in person.
- **Party affiliation:** to vote in a party's primary you must be registered with that party by the deadline set each spring. In 2026 it was May 13.
