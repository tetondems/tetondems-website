## How Voters can register

-   **In person** at the County Clerk’s office during regular hours (8am–5pm)


-   **By mail** using the Wyoming voter registration form

    -   Complete forms found [here (must be notarized)](https://sos.wyo.gov/Elections/State/RegisteringToVote.aspx)

-   **At the polls on Election Day** if not registered in advance


* * *

## Cutoff / special periods
**If** registering during:

-   **Aug 4 - Aug 17, 2026** (before Primary), or

-   **Oct 20 - Nov 2, 2026** (before General)

    You must **register and vote at the** **same time**


* * *

## Address or party changes:

-   Must be done **in person** after registration closes

-   ***currently registered voters*** to declare or change their political party is **May 13th, 2026**
